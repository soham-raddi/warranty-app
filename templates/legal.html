Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ '2026-10-19' }}

What Digital Twin stores

  • Your Google account name, email address and profile picture, used to sign you in.
  • The receipts, warranty cards and item details you or your family upload, and any service history you add.
  • Your workspace settings, membership and invite records, and the messages you send to the assistant.

How it is used

Your data is used only to provide the app: showing your inventory, working out warranty dates and answering your questions. It is never sold or used for advertising.

Who can see it

Items, receipts and settings in a workspace are visible only to that workspace's members. Your assistant chats are visible only to you.

Services we rely on

  • Google for sign-in.
  • Groq reads receipt and warranty card images and answers the assistant's questions, so it receives those images and, when you chat, your inventory and messages.
    • {% if email_enabled %}
  • Resend delivers invite and warranty reminder emails, so it receives the recipient's email address and the item names in the reminder.
    • {% endif %}
  • Supabase stores the database and uploaded files; Vercel hosts the app.

Keeping or deleting your data

You can export a workspace's inventory as CSV, and delete a workspace or your whole account at any time from Settings → Workspace & members. Deleting removes the related items, files and chats.

Terms of Use

Last updated: {{ '2026-10-19' }}

Using Digital Twin

Digital Twin helps you keep track of purchases, receipts and warranties. Use it only for documents you are allowed to share, and keep your Google account secure.

AI-read details

Receipt details and warranty dates are read automatically and can be wrong. Check important details (such as warranty end dates) against the original documents before relying on them.

Fair use

Limits apply to receipt scans, assistant messages and uploads to keep the service available for everyone. Accounts used for abuse may be removed.

No warranty for the app itself

The app is provided as-is, without guarantees of availability. Keep your own copies of important documents.
